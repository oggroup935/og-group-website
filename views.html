FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

How do I get on the list?+
Join the Investor Network and define your buy-box. That's all it takes to start receiving matched deals.
What's a buy-box?+
Your criteria: the areas, price range, condition, and strategy you buy in. We score every deal against it.
Will you blast my info?+
Never. Contact details are protected server-side, and you only receive deals that fit your buy-box.
Are these real deals?+
Yes — truth-only. Every deal passes a human quality gate with real comps before it reaches you.
When is the self-serve portal coming?+
Soon. It will let you browse and reserve matched deals yourself, directly in the app.
Who can partner with OG GROUP?+
Wholesalers and acquisition reps working the Cleveland market who control their own contracts and run straight with sellers. Partnership is invite-based while we onboard the first operators.
Do I get access to the system itself?+
Yes — an account in OG OS with the field map, the comps engine, the AI rehab underwrite and the deal pipeline. Not a login to a watered-down portal: the system our own acquisitions run on.
Who owns the deal and the relationship?+
You do. Attribution stays with whoever brought the deal, and buyer contact details are protected at the server so nobody can go around you.
What gets a deal rejected from the board?+
Missing comps, undocumented condition, numbers that don't hold up, or a contract you don't actually control. The gate is the same one our own deals pass.
How do splits work?+
Agreed up front, per deal, and tracked in the system rather than in someone's memory. We'll walk through the specifics on the first call.
